                    Quick help

                    A little help finding your way around Cosmifold.

                    Getting started

                    Think of a Galaxy as one big area of your life: a trip, a class, recipes, a home project. Add items inside it and Cosmifold handles the space-y part.

                    Not sure where to start? Choose a Starter Galaxy: Blank, Trip, Home project, Class / course, Recipes, or Photos / inspiration. Change anything you want.

                    Things look different as you go deeper. Bigger bodies are higher-level items; smaller ones are details inside them. You don't need to manage that yourself.

                    Moving around

                    Drag empty space to move around. Scroll, use your trackpad, or pinch to zoom. The + and − buttons zoom too.

                    Double-click or double-tap an item to focus on it.

                    Lost in space? Hit Fit to bring the Galaxy back into view.

                    Adding your stuff

                    The + beside Universe creates a Galaxy. The + beside an item in the sidebar lets you add something inside it.

                    Got a document, photo, video, or link? Drop it onto an item, on the canvas or in the sidebar. Photos and videos go into Media, documents into Files, and links become Bookmarks. Media, Files, Notes, and Bookmarks stay with that item.

                    Dragging through the sidebar? Pause over a collapsed branch and it opens so you can reach the items inside.

                    Constellations

                    Sometimes things belong together even though they live in different places. A Constellation pulls those items together without moving them.

                    Try it for a trip, a project, or "stuff I need this week." Use the + beside Constellations to start one. An item can belong to more than one.

                    Archiving old Galaxies

                    Done with a Galaxy for now? Choose Archive Galaxy from its menu instead of deleting it.

                    It disappears from your active Universe, but everything stays safe. Restore it anytime from Archived Galaxies in the sidebar.

                    A few useful tricks

                    Search finds items across your active Universe. Right-click an item or open its ••• menu for extra actions.

                    Make a checklist in Notes by typing:

                    - [ ] Pack passport
- [x] Book hotel

                    When reading a Note, click or tap its boxes to check things off.

                    Using the same item in two places

                    Need the same thing somewhere else too? Open the destination item's + menu in the sidebar and choose Add existing item.

                    It's still the same item. Changes you make show everywhere.

                    Remove from here removes only that extra appearance. It doesn't delete the original. Go to original takes you back to where it lives.
